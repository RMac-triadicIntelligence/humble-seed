Author findings on v0.8.7, from post-freeze probes (author_probe_v0_8_7.py -> AUTHOR_PROBE_v0_8_7.txt).
Sealed before any reviewer has seen v0.8.7.

A1 (low-medium) Restore forgets the owner's answers, and the owner cannot repeat them (P1).
   Restore discards every event after the attested checkpoint. That includes the organism's copy of any
   owner answer given since, and every pending reading. The owner's ledger keeps those answers.
   P1:
   - the owner says 'no' to reading Y;
   - an unrelated crossing is applied, then restored away;
   - a new verbosity reading Z is issued; the organism applies it (it no longer sees the 'no');
   - attest refuses it ("its latest answer in verbosity since its record began is 'no'");
   - the owner cannot answer Y again ("No pending reading with that id": restore cleared it).
   The owner's 'no' is honoured, so there is no unauthorized state. But the first crossing in that category
   after a restore is wasted, and getting out needs another restore plus an answer to a new reading.
   Fix direction: restore carries the discarded owner_answer events (and their basin evidence) into the restored
   trace after the "restored" event. They are non-authorizing.
A2 (low) A reader callback that calls an owner operation can deadlock against attest in another thread (P5).
   v0.8.7 runs the reader under the organism lock (R35). Lock order is otherwise owner -> organism. A reader that
   calls an owner operation (P5: sign_proposal) takes them organism -> owner. With another thread inside attest
   (owner lock held, waiting for the organism lock), both threads wait forever. P5: neither finished within 5 s.
   v0.8.6 did not hold the organism lock during the reader, so this is new in v0.8.7.
   Fix direction: refuse an owner operation from a thread that already holds an organism lock, or document the
   order and enforce it.
A3 (low) A symlinked path to the same organism deadlocks one thread against itself (P6).
   Locks are keyed by os.path.abspath, which does not resolve symlinks. Inside an operation on an object opened
   at the real path, a callback writes through an object opened via a symlink to the same directory. The
   in-thread reentrancy does not apply (a different key), and flock on a second descriptor of the same file
   blocks. P6 timed out after 20 s. Across processes, serialization still holds.
   Fix direction: key the lock registry by os.path.realpath.
A4 (note) Cost. ms per dwell, back to back on one machine:

   | Trace length | v0.8.6 | v0.8.7 |
   |---|---|---|
   | ~22 | 2.9 | 2.6 |
   | ~500 | 11.5 | 13.7 |
   | ~2000 | 41.0 | 46.9 |

   About 15% at 2000 entries, within run-to-run noise at small sizes. Kept restore files accumulate, one
   per restore, with no limit (P2).

Confirmed as documented, not findings:
   P2 (R). Restoring twice in a row works. Restoring through a stale object works (from disk), and that object
       is then current. An object loaded before the restore is refused as stale.
   P3 (Q across processes). 4 processes x 50 dwells plus 10 attests from a 5th: 200 of 200 dwellings saved,
       witness 10, fresh audit True.
   P4 (Q, mixed threads). Dwell, cross+attest, answer and audit threads ran for 6 s on one object:
       - 204 dwells, 42 crossings, 83 answers;
       - saved checkpoint 42 = witness 42; fresh audit True;
       - the audit thread stopped on IntegrityError when it caught the organism between an apply and its
         attest. An unattested state does not verify (T17); audit is not meant to pass there.
   P8 (R36). A basin edited on disk (resealed) is carried through restore, and the result loads. The basin is
       unattested evidence (R36), and the edit loaded before the restore as well (R30).
